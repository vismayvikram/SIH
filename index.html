Data Provenance & Cadastral Notice

Important Provenance Declaration:
“Project Vaayu sample; repository README describes underlying data as Ministry-supplied SVAMITVA/hackathon material. Provenance is reported, not independently verified. Not official parcel ground truth.”

Not an Official Cadastre: This prototype is built for geospatial review and topology conflict inspection. The 317 building footprint annotations represent physical rooftop boundaries and are NOT cadastral parcels.

Zero Cadastral Parcels: Official self-service cadastral parcel GIS vectors do not exist for Lalpur Village (LGD 511638). The parcel layer template is intentionally empty (0 features) and no fictional parcel boundaries have been fabricated.

Privacy Sanitization: All personal identities, owner names, property IDs, and property card numbers have been completely stripped from the working assets.

OpenStreetMap Attribution: Centerline vectors and basemap tiles are distributed under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL 1.0) by © OpenStreetMap contributors.
